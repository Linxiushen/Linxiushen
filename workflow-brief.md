# Start a workflow conversation

Copy the questions below into an email to **[m15178824683@gmail.com](mailto:m15178824683@gmail.com?subject=AI%20workflow%20assessment%20inquiry)**. A few sentences are enough for the first discussion.

1. What recurring task would you like to improve, and who owns it?
2. What are the two main tools involved?
3. Where does the work get stuck, and what should the final result look like?
4. How often does the task happen? An estimate is fine; label it as an estimate.
5. What is your time zone, and when could you meet in English?

Use a general description. Please leave out confidential client data, credentials and private files. If more detail is needed, we will agree how to share it.

The first exchange establishes fit and a meeting time. A paid assessment or implementation starts only after we agree its scope, fees, timing and terms.

# Connecting design and office workflows through Codex

An anonymized overview of a delivered enterprise project.

## Starting point

Image work used specialist AI design SaaS. The implementation built on those tools and connected them with business workflows through Codex.

## Delivered change

Codex was introduced as a shared entry point for AI work. Design SaaS and office workflows were connected through this approach, while company-specific procedures were captured in Codex Skills. Information flows connected work across business platforms.

The change brought specialist software and reusable company procedures together: work could be organized through a common entry point, with Skills capturing instructions for recurring tasks.

## What this suggests for a new engagement

For a team that already uses AI software, a useful starting point is one recurring workflow and the tools it needs. An assessment can identify the input, the system operations, the required output and who decides whether the work is acceptable.

A proposed pilot then defines the company-specific Skill, its supporting tools, evaluation examples, review points, exception handling and handover to a named owner. These are proposed deliverables for a new engagement; their exact scope is agreed with the client.

## Scope of this account

This account is based on the practitioner's description of a delivered project and is shared with permission. It describes the project-level workflow change. Quantitative business outcomes, individual responsibility breakdowns, internal interfaces and client-identifying material are outside this account.

The overview does not imply endorsement by a software provider. Product permissions, system connections and operating responsibilities are defined for each implementation.
